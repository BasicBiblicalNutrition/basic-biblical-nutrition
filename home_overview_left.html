What is Biblical Functional Nutrition?

Biblical Functional Nutrition combines modern science with timeless biblical principles to better understand the connections between food, lifestyle, and health. It recognizes that our bodies are interconnected systems, influenced not only by genetics, but also by our environment, nutrition, lifestyle, and epigenetics. Biblical nutrition draws inspiration from the whole, nourishing foods found throughout Scripture and the agricultural traditions of the ancient Mediterranean and Near East, while Functional Nutrition asks how those foods may support the unique needs of each individual. Together, faith and science provide a framework for understanding health as both personal and interconnected, while respecting God's design.

Guided by Faith, Governed by Nature

We believe our bodies are temples, deserving of nourishment that honours our Creator. Biblical Functional Nutrition brings together faith, food, and science to explore how the body's systems work together rather than viewing health concerns in isolation. Functional Nutrition looks at the relationships among digestion, nutrition, inflammation, genetics, epigenetics, lifestyle, and the body's natural processes. Because each person is unique, our responses to food and lifestyle can differ. Guided by faith and informed by science, BBN seeks practical ways to nourish the body wisely, support its God-given design, and make choices that promote health for the long term.
