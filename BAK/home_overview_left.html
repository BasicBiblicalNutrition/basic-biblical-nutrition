What is Biblical Functional Nutrition?

Biblical Functional Nutrition combines modern science with timeless biblical principles to better understand the connections between food, lifestyle, and health. It recognizes that our bodies are interconnected systems, influenced not only by genetics, but also by our environment, nutrition, lifestyle, and epigenetics—the processes that can influence how our genes are expressed without changing our DNA. Biblical nutrition draws inspiration from the whole, nourishing foods found throughout Scripture and the agricultural traditions of the ancient Mediterranean and Near East, while Functional Nutrition asks how those foods may support the unique needs of each individual. Together, faith and science provide a framework for understanding health as both personal and interconnected, while respecting God's design.
