Take 2... and Call Me in the Morning?

Why “Take 2”?

Take 2 is a place where you can ask a question about nutrition, supplements, nutrients, or healthy living. Each week, we’ll choose one question to research and answer right here on the BBN website.

Why “Take 2”? I’m glad you asked!

The name has two meanings:

1. “Take 2 and call me in the morning.” is a familiar running joke about what you might hear when you visit the doctor.

2. In the film industry, a “Take 2” is the second recorded attempt at a particular shot or scene. Sometimes the first take goes well, but the director wants to try something different — a new emotional delivery, different movement or blocking, or another camera angle.

And that’s the idea behind BBN’s Take 2: Ask the question. Take another look. Dig into the research. See what we uncover.
